# Design Context

## Users and context

The primary audience is a general, non-technical supporter—often a Pakistani donor abroad or a family member in Lahore—who arrives with limited time, wants to understand the school quickly, and needs confidence about who operates the project and who receives a payment. Secondary users include visitors, guardians, school families, and reviewers checking the public record.

## Jobs to be done

- Understand what Hammad Foundation is and where the school operates.
- See the concrete relationship between a contribution and school support.
- Choose a one-time or monthly support option without guessing.
- Verify the YZ Educational Services payment relationship before continuing.
- Find a direct, human route to the school team for questions or a campus visit.

## Brand personality

Grounded, direct, humane, accountable, and locally specific. The interface should feel like a well-run school record with a warm front door—not like a campaign landing page or a financial dashboard.

## Emotional goal

Move the visitor from “I need to understand this” to “I can trust the next step.” The visual language should create calm urgency: the waiting list is real and important, but the experience never pressures visitors with spectacle.

## Aesthetic direction

Warm editorial utility: paper-toned neutrals, deep charcoal ink, one restrained school green, generous margins, asymmetric but stable grids, strong typographic hierarchy, and real-world imagery treated with dignity. Use structure, whitespace, and evidence labels as the memorable focal point.

## Accessibility constraints

- Body copy remains at least 16px on reading surfaces and uses readable line lengths.
- Interactive targets are at least 44px high.
- Visible focus rings use the green accent and remain present for keyboard users.
- Color is never the only signal for status.
- Images use descriptive alt text and fixed aspect-ratio containers to reduce layout shift.
- Motion is limited to opacity and transforms and is disabled when prefers-reduced-motion is enabled.
- Mobile layouts collapse to one column below 768px with no horizontal scrolling.

## Design principles

1. Make the operating relationship visible before asking for support.
2. Use one clear focal point per section; let evidence and whitespace carry emphasis.
3. Prefer borders, rhythm, and typography over decorative effects and excessive cards.
4. Keep urgency human and specific; never manufacture proof or use filler copy.
5. Every action should state what happens next.
